Initial release of ezmpv.

- libmpv-based audio and video playback
- Background playback with media-session controls
- Mini player and full Now Playing screen with shared-element morph
- User playlists and Favorites
- Library tab scanning bookmarked folders, organized by artist/album/year
- Browse tab with SAF file browser and bookmarks
- Audio-only mode for video
- Play-sequence modes including shuffle and Casino (true random)
